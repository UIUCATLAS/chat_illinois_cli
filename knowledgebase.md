# BLS Admissions

The Bachelor of Liberal Studies (BLS) is designed for transfer and reentry students who are ready to complete their degree fully online.

## Requirements for Transfer Application

To be considered for admission, you must:

- Have at least 12 graded, transferable credit hours at the time of enrollment.
- Hold a minimum 2.0 cumulative GPA (on a 4.0 scale) based on all graded, transferable coursework.
- Provide all previous transcripts and proof of high school completion or GED.
- Complete the essays on the application.

## Students Returning to Illinois (Reentry)

If you are a student who has previously attended Illinois, you must:

- Be inactive for two semesters or more.
- Complete the LAS Re-Entry Application form instead of the traditional transfer application.

**Note:** Admission is contingent upon additional factors, which may include academic standing rules and performance patterns prior to leaving and before reentry.

## Program Options

### Multiple Start Dates

Apply for fall, summer, or spring terms.

### Flexible Enrollment

Part-time study is available.

## Contact

- Program website: <https://lasonline.illinois.edu/programs/bls>
- Email: [onlineBLS@illinois.edu](mailto:onlineBLS@illinois.edu)

## Concentrations

BLS students will choose at least one concentration.

### Global Perspectives: 15 credit hours

Provides a broad, interdisciplinary understanding of global issues while developing students' critical thinking, communication skills, and ability to analyze complex challenges and propose solutions.

- ESE 320: Water Planet, Water Crisis (3)
- ESE 467: Multimedia Environmental Communications (3)
- GLBL 340: Global Health: Policy & Governance (3)
- HIST 311: Global History of Intelligence (3)
- SOC 364: Impacts of Globalization (3)

### Health and Society: 15 credit hours

Explores how social, cultural, and environmental factors shape health, building students' critical thinking, communication skills, and understanding of the individual-community connection.

- ANTH 249: Evolution and Human Disease (3)
- GLBL 340: Global Health: Policy & Governance (3)
- GLBL 440: Global Health: Interventions & Evaluations (3)
- HK 302: Disability in American Society (3)
- IB 411: Bioinspiration (3)

### Management Studies: 14-15 credit hours

Provides key learning in business fundamentals, leadership, and organizational behavior, strengthening students' critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills.

Required courses:

- CMN 212: Intro to Organizational Communication (3)
- EPOL 474: Diversity in the Workplace (3)
- LER 300: Workers, Unions, and Politics (3)

Select two of the following:

- ADV 320: Sale Management (3)
- LEAD 425: Leading Teams (3)
- LEAD 427: Human-centered Organizational Leadership and Management (2)

# BLS Fast Facts

*Updated Fall 2025*

## Program Overview

- **Degree type:** Bachelor of Liberal Studies (BLS)
- **Administered by:** College of Liberal Arts & Sciences (LAS)
- **Delivery mode:** 100% online, asynchronous
- **Audience:** Adult learners, transfer students, and degree completers
- **Enrollment options:** Part-time or full-time
- **Goal:** Provide a flexible, interdisciplinary path to complete a bachelor's degree that delivers both a strong liberal arts foundation and practical, transferable skills needed in today's workforce.

**Launch date: Fall 2026**

## Admissions

- **Applicants:** Transfer or reentry students only
  - ICT and reentry students must be inactive for 2 semesters.
- **Minimum credit hours:** 12, with a 2.0 minimum cumulative GPA

## Curriculum

- **Total credits required:** 120 hours
- **Major coursework:**
  - General Education courses (follows campus guidelines; standard substitutions, transfer credit, and high school equivalencies may count toward these requirements)
  - Major curriculum: 16 courses, 44 required hours
  - Choose a concentration: Health and Society, Global Perspectives, or Management Studies
- **Learning outcomes:** Graduates of the BLS program will demonstrate:
  - Strong critical thinking and problem-solving skills
  - Effective written and oral communication
  - Understanding of individuals and communities in global contexts
  - Leadership and organizational skills through applied coursework

## Tuition

- **Tuition rate:** $433 per credit hour (AY25-26)
  - No in-state/out-of-state distinction; tuition is charged at a flat online rate.
  - Billing is per credit hour enrolled each semester.
  - The University of Illinois employee tuition waiver is accepted if the applicant meets waiver eligibility requirements.

*Tuition = (Number of Courses x Credits per Course) x $433*

- **Fee:** $3 per billing hour (Online Service Fee)
  - Covers services from the Office of the Dean of Students, the library, technology services, and counseling services.

**No other campus-based fees are charged.**
